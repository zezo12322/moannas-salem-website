# مؤنث سالم — Visual Direction Moodboard
**Concept:** Safe Notebook · Support Room · Advocacy Platform

---

## CORE VISUAL CONCEPT

The visual language draws from three overlapping metaphors:

**1. The Notebook** — A private, protected space where words matter. Pages, lines, ink marks, handwriting textures. The act of writing as testimony, as evidence, as power.

**2. The Room** — A physical space that feels warm, contained, and safe. Soft lighting, close distances, the sense of being heard. Not a public square — a confidential room.

**3. The Platform** — When testimonies leave the room, they become public knowledge and collective voice. Bold typography, clear data, organized information — strength without aggression.

## COLOR PALETTE

| Role | Hex | Description |
|------|-----|-------------|
| Deep Feminist Purple | `#4B245E` | Primary authority — headers, CTAs, hero blocks |
| Warm Rose Clay | `#C66B7D` | Warmth — highlights, hover states, accents |
| Safe Peach | `#F2B6A0` | Softness — card backgrounds, illustration fills |
| Soft Paper | `#FAF5EF` | Main background — warm white, like paper |
| Ink Black | `#211A22` | Body text — deep, readable, not harsh |
| Trust Green | `#4F7C68` | Confirmation — privacy badges, success states |
| Care Amber | `#D9963D` | Attention — warnings only, used sparingly |

**Palette feeling:** Warm, serious, readable. Not corporate. Not aggressive. Not generically pink. The purple anchors credibility; the peach and rose add human warmth; the paper background gives it intimacy over polish.

## TYPOGRAPHY DIRECTION

### Arabic (Primary Language)
- **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** — Primary workhorse. Modern, clear, humanist. Works well at all sizes. Strong Arabic character shapes without feeling cold.
- **Cairo** — Alternative for display headings. Slightly more personality.
- **Noto Sans Arabic** — Fallback / body at small sizes.

### English (Secondary / Institutional)
- **Inter** — Clean, functional. For English UI elements, metadata.
- **IBM Plex Sans** — Matches IBM Plex Sans Arabic aesthetically.

### Typographic Scale (Arabic)
- H1: 42–52px, weight 700, tight leading
- H2: 32–36px, weight 600
- H3: 24px, weight 600
- Body: 17–18px, weight 400, generous line height (1.8–2.0 for Arabic)
- Small/label: 13–14px, weight 500
- Microcopy (form notes): 12px, Trust Green or muted

## ILLUSTRATION STYLE

**Preferred direction:**
- Flat line art with selective solid fills
- Hands — holding pens, notebooks, microphones, phones
- Abstract figures — silhouettes, protected forms, never exposed faces
- Paper textures overlaid on geometric shapes
- Dots and marks as metaphors for voices, testimonies, data points
- Pen strokes that feel handmade within a clean layout

**Color use in illustrations:**
- Primary fills: Safe Peach `#F2B6A0` + Deep Purple `#4B245E`
- Outlines: Ink Black `#211A22` at reduced opacity
- Accent marks: Warm Rose Clay `#C66B7D`

**Do not use:**
- Stock photography of crying women
- Images suggesting vulnerability, victimhood, or helplessness
- Exposed faces without consent
- Court/police/legal imagery that feels threatening
- Purely decorative geometric patterns with no meaning

## PHOTOGRAPHY DIRECTION (where used)

- Warm-toned, natural light
- Hands on keyboards, notebooks, microphones — close shots
- Blurred / silhouetted figures in professional settings
- Group solidarity shots (women together, not isolated)
- Workspaces: newsrooms, desks, notebooks
- Never: stock "empowerment" fist imagery, forced smiles, dramatic lighting

## LOGO DIRECTION

### Concepts to explore:

**Option A — Protected Pen**
A pen (ق / qalam metaphor) enclosed within a soft circular form. The circle = protection, the pen = voice. Arabic letterform of "م" (meem) from مؤنث can anchor it.

**Option B — Testimony Dots**
Multiple dots arranged in a flowing pattern, each dot = a voice/testimony. Dots converge into the institution name. Suggests collective, not individual.

**Option C — Quotation Frame**
Arabic opening quotation mark (الشَّوْلَة) transformed into a protective enclosure. The name sits within it. References testimony, storytelling, testimony.

**Option D — Notebook Corner**
A folded notebook corner forms the logo mark. Clean, functional, meaningful. The fold = a page being turned, a story beginning.

**Recommended direction:** Option A or B. Both are uniquely feminist without being stereotypical, work at small sizes, and carry clear meaning.

### Logo usage:
- Full color on Soft Paper background
- White version on Deep Purple backgrounds
- Single-color (purple) for documents and stamps
- Avoid placing logo on complex photographic backgrounds

## UI COMPONENT FEELING

- Rounded corners (12–16px) — approachable, not sharp or institutional
- Generous padding inside cards (24–32px)
- Subtle shadows (no harsh drop shadows) — soft elevation
- Form fields: clear borders, ample height (48px), generous labels above
- Privacy badges: Trust Green with lock icon, always visible near forms
- CTAs: filled for primary (purple), outlined for secondary, text-link for tertiary

---

## REFERENCE AESTHETIC DIRECTION

Think of a blend between:
- A well-designed Arabic feminist magazine (serious, beautiful)
- A digital rights organization's website (trustworthy, clear)
- A therapeutic support platform (warm, private, non-alarming)

**Not:** A government site, an international NGO's template, a social media brand, a fashion/beauty brand.

---

## MOTION / INTERACTION DIRECTION

- Subtle — nothing animated that distracts from reading or seeking help
- Smooth scroll, fade-ins on scroll for content sections
- Form submissions: clear loading state + success/error messages (no page reload)
- Hover on cards: slight elevation + warm rose underlay
- No auto-playing videos or sounds
- Transition speed: 200–300ms, ease-in-out — feels intentional, not flashy
